About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small family kitchen with one oven and a big dream. Neighbors kept asking for more loaves, so we opened our doors on Lantern Street. Today we are still a small bakery, and we still bake every item by hand for the people who live and work around us.

See Behind the Scenes

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good bread starts with good ingredients. We buy our flour from regional mills, our eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and our fruit from local growers whenever the season allows. Keeping our suppliers close helps us bake fresher food and supports the community that supports us.
